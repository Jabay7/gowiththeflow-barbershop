The shop

A neighborhood barbershop that treats you like family

Go With The Flow is a Latino-owned shop on Mission Ave run by Flo, a barber who has spent years building a loyal following one chair at a time. Military welcome, kids welcome, first-timers welcome.

  1. Barbers who listen. Tell us what you want, show us a photo, or say “same as last time.” You leave with the cut you came for.
  2. Clean, relaxed, no rush. A clean chair, a good conversation if you want one, and quiet if you do not.
  3. Easy to get to. Downtown Oceanside, minutes from the pier and Camp Pendleton, with free parking out front.
